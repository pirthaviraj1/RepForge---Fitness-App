# RepForge — Fitness & Workout App 💪
![RepForge Fitness App](repforge-logo.png)

**Forge your strength. Build better habits. Become your best self.**

RepForge is a fitness app designed to help users improve their fitness journey with exercise guidance, workout routines, diet tips, and useful fitness features — all in one place.

## ✨ About RepForge

RepForge aims to make fitness easier, more engaging, and more accessible. Whether you're starting your fitness journey or looking to improve your existing routine, RepForge brings helpful fitness resources together in one app.

## 🚀 Features

- **Exercise Guidance** — Explore exercises to support your fitness goals.
- **Workout Routines** — Discover workout ideas and build a consistent exercise habit.
- **Diet & Nutrition Tips** — Learn helpful diet tips and healthy eating habits.
- **Fitness Resources** — Find useful information to support your fitness journey.
- **User-Friendly Experience** — Designed to make fitness features easy to explore.
- **All-in-One Fitness Hub** — Bring workouts, exercise guidance, and nutrition tips together.

*Note: Available features may depend on the current implementation of the app.*

## 🎯 Our Goal

The goal of RepForge is to help users stay motivated, develop healthier habits, and make their fitness journey more organized and enjoyable.
